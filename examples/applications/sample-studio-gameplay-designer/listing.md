# Sample Studio: Gameplay Designer, Combat

Fictional listing for the demo.

Sample Studio is making a four-player co-op action game in Unreal Engine 5. We're looking for a Gameplay Designer to own enemies and encounters from first prototype to final polish.

## What you'll do

- Design enemy behaviors and boss encounters that play well solo and in a full party
- Build and balance player abilities with the systems team
- Prototype quickly in Blueprints and iterate from playtest feedback
- Tune difficulty scaling for one to four players
- Work closely with animation, VFX, and engineering

## What you bring

- 4+ years designing combat or action gameplay
- Shipped at least one title
- Strong Unreal Engine and Blueprint skills
- Comfortable using playtest data to make tuning calls

## Nice to have

- Live-service or seasonal content experience
- Console development experience
- Experience mentoring other designers
